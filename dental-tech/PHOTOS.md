# Photos

Put photos in `src/photos/` with these exact names, then run `python3 build.py`.
Until a photo exists, its spot shows a soft placeholder with a camera icon.

**Format:** JPG straight from the phone, at least 2000 px, bright daylight, sent by email or Drive (Viber and WhatsApp shrink photos).
Aim for a modern dental brand look: bright, clean, friendly. Giorgos smiling, a tidy clinic behind him, no clutter.
