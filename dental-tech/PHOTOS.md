# Photos

Put photos in `src/photos/` with these exact names, then run `python3 build.py`.
Until a photo exists, its spot shows a soft mint placeholder.

**Format:** JPG straight from the phone, at least 2000 px, bright daylight, sent by email or Drive (Viber and WhatsApp shrink photos).
The look is a modern dental brand, so aim for bright, clean and friendly: Giorgos smiling, a tidy clinic in the background, no clutter.

| File | What to shoot | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Giorgos at a dental chair, smiling at the camera, tools in hand. The most important photo. | Portrait, roughly 4:4.4 |
| `giorgos.jpg` | A friendly portrait of Giorgos, from the waist up, with a plain or clinic background | Portrait 4:5 |
| `s-mixanimata.jpg` | A dental chair or unit, clean and well lit. His own work, or free stock such as [this one on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-dental-room-with-a-blue-chair-and-dental-equipment-AQG6C_xYEpA) | Square-ish |

Never use a stock photo of a person in place of Giorgos.
Free stock is fine for equipment (Unsplash, Pexels; avoid "Unsplash+", which is paid).
