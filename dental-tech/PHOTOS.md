# Photos

Put photos in `src/photos/` with these exact names, then run `python3 build.py`.
Until a photo is there, its spot shows a plain marble block, so you can add them one by one.

**Format:** JPG straight from the phone, at least 2000 px, sent by email or Drive (Viber and WhatsApp shrink photos).
A PNG with a transparent background also works; the build keeps the transparency.
